Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Task List is a personal portfolio project. This page explains what information it uses when you sign in with Google.

What we collect

  • From your Google account: your Google account ID, name, and email address. These are used only to sign you in and show who is logged in.
  • Your tasks: the text, date, and completed status of tasks you create, stored together with your Google account ID so only you can see them.

How it is stored

  • Your name and email are kept in a sign-in cookie in your browser for up to one week. They are not saved in the database.
  • Tasks are stored in a MongoDB Atlas database.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, share, or use your information for advertising.
  • We do not access your Google contacts, files, or any other Google data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your tasks at any time inside the app. Logging out removes the sign-in cookie. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through github.com/rmvv03.
